9. APP SCREENS AND BEHAVIOR

Admin navigation: Overview, Inquiries, Onboarding, Players, Parents, Coaches, Locations, Batches, Sessions, Attendance, Courses, Assessments, Subscriptions, Payments, Settings.

Overview: real pending requests, follow-ups, today's sessions, subscriptions needing review and unpaid recorded charges. Empty means zero and useful setup actions. Query failure is an error, not zero.

Players: search/filter by status/location/batch. Detail tabs: Profile, Subscription, Schedule & Attendance, Skills/Progress, Payments. Courses must also have a separate navigation screen.
Parents: linked children and verified account linkage; review changed child links explicitly. Parent-as-player works without duplicate login.
Coaches/locations/batches: create/edit/archive and manage assignments; archives retain historical records but disallow new allocations.

Sessions: calendar/list, group/one-to-one, coach/location/batch, capacity, allocations and written plan. Validate end after start, duplicates, capacity and coach/player time conflicts. Recurrence creates identifiable instances without duplicates; distinguish editing one instance from future instances. Cancellations stay in history and do not consume session allowance.
Attendance: quick mobile roster; bulk mark plus per-player correction. Default Unmarked, not Present. Only allocated players unless admin adds allocation. Persist pending/saved/error states and audit changes.

Courses: owner creates courses/levels/lessons, orders/publishes them, assigns players; coach records progress. Start empty. No invented curriculum or promotion thresholds.
Assessments: select assigned player, date, optional session/course/lesson and configured active rubric. Render typed inputs from configuration. With no rubric, show Scoring format needs configuration; disable scored publishing but allow draft notes. Owner-only rubric builder configures criteria/types/ranges/options/optional weights. Version rubrics; past scores keep original meaning. Charts/totals only when comparable records and defined calculation rules exist. Never average unrelated scales. Members see published results only.

Subscriptions: owner configures plans and assigns actual dates/fee/allowance. Lifecycle is separate from payment state. No automatic renewal/billing. No supplied deduction policy: default to no automatic deductions; provide owner configuration for scheduled/present-session consumption or other explicit rule. Deduction/reversal must be traceable and idempotent. Do not invent no-show fees, grace periods or refunds.
Payments: implement manual records first (cash/bank transfer/UPI/other), amount/date/reference and linked player/charge. Compute due/paid/partial balances from posted records. Correct with reversal/adjustment, not erased history. A screenshot or payment intent is not verified gateway settlement.
Online gateway: prepare integration boundary, disabled until an actual provider is configured. No fake checkout success. Future integration uses server-side secrets, signature-validated webhooks and idempotent settlement updates.

Settings: contacts, approved biographies/images, public content, setup checklist, plans, attendance/usage rules and rubrics. Staff login emails are not automatically public contacts.
Member portal: child-switcher or own profile, upcoming allocated sessions, attendance, subscription/payment summary, published progress. No private notes, unpublished assessments or other families. No public player pages.
