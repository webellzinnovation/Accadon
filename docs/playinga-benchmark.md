# Playinga public feature benchmark

Checked 2026-10-15. This is a first-party product benchmark, not a hands-on verification or an independent audit. Scope is one named competitor, so four relevant first-party pages were used rather than padding the research with unrelated reviews.

## Sources read

- https://playinga.com/en/tournament-management-software/ : tournament microsites, multi-sport/division registrations, custom fields/consent, withdrawal, integrated fee collection, email templates/own SMTP, fixture formats, seeding/byes, drag-and-drop court/venue scheduling, score approval, standings/tiebreakers, team play, galleries, sponsor placements and roles.
- https://playinga.com/en/ : public positioning across tournaments, clubs, venues, teams, player profiles, scoring and scheduling.
- https://playinga.com/en/pricing/ : organization/team/tournament tiers, finance/reporting, communications and separate venue-management add-ons. Pricing FAQ has inconsistent plan names and allowances, so no prices, limits or tier equivalence have been copied into Accadon.
- https://playinga.com/en/free-sports-scheduler/ : registration, fixtures, brackets, scheduling and sports-specific scoring.

The guessed club-management URL returned CRAWL_NOT_FOUND. No conclusion about missing club features was drawn from that failure. The homepage and pricing page provide the club/organization context used here.

## Mirrored as Accadon design concepts

1. Event setup, multi-sport divisions, registration review/consent and entry lifecycle.
2. Fixture formats, seeds/byes, draw progression, match desk and score approval.
3. League tables, sport-specific point rules and tie-break transparency.
4. Player/team rosters, profile access and staff scope.
5. Court/venue planning and overlapping-slot review.
6. Notices/communication drafts and explicit audience selection.
7. Finance/report categories, adapted to the academy's own gateway.

## Accadon-specific depth retained

Coaching batches/session plans/attendance; parent and linked-player view; configurable course/level/lesson/chapter structure and assessments; match journal; academy subscriptions/charges/manual records; tenant-scoped admin and separate platform oversight. These come from Accadon's owner direction, not a claim that Playinga offers them or lacks them.

## Deliberately not copied

- Playinga's integrated platform collection/settlement or transaction pricing. Accadon uses each academy's gateway.
- Exact free/pro entitlements, team/event caps, processing fees, subscription prices or add-on prices.
- Sponsor logos, testimonials, customer counts, ad-free/white-label guarantees, galleries or external artwork.
- Public venue discovery/rental marketplace. Accadon preview is internal court scheduling only.
- Automatic email/SMS/WhatsApp/push delivery or published notices without configured services.
- Public child profiles, personal sports social network, instant access or unverified guardian links.
- Unexplained sports analytics or invented scores/progress/financial charts.
