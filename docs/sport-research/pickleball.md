# Pickleball
## Decision for Accadon
Ship explicit traditional side-out and USA Pickleball 2026 provisional rally profiles. Never treat 'pickleball scoring' as one generic counter. Doubles side-out needs player identity, starting server and service turn; rally scoring has a different service lifecycle. In India, store sanctioning body, rating provider and event regulations separately: an AIPA document, IPA event and DUPR rating are not interchangeable authorities. [P1-P5]
Research checked 15 October 2026. Rules below use the 2026 USA Pickleball Official Rulebook, with its new section numbering. An Indian organiser must explicitly adopt the relevant rulebook and local variations. This is not a claim that every Indian event follows USA Pickleball tournament regulations.
## 1. Rules and score anatomy
- Standard singles is one player per side; doubles two. Traditional scoring awards a point only when the serving side wins a rally. Losing a rally while receiving does not change the opponent's service right; winning while receiving transfers service/turn as applicable without scoring. Tournament match formats include best two of three to 11, best three of five to 11, or one game to 15/21; one game to 11 has the specified round-robin eligibility restriction. All tournament scoring options are win by two except the permitted win-by-one team-play variation. No general hard cap is stated. [P1: 4, 5, 15.C]
- Traditional singles: serve right at even server scores, left at odd. Server winning alternates serving areas and continues; losing produces a side-out. Singles score call has two numbers: server's score then receiver's. [P1: 5.A, 6.B]
- Traditional doubles: normally both players serve before side-out. At the start of each game, only one starting player serves, designated the second server for that opening service rotation. Thus opening call is 0-0-2. Three-number call is serving-team points, receiving-team points, server number 1 or 2. The last number is a turn designation, not a permanently assigned player number. [P1: 5.B, 6.B]
- At each later side-out, the correctly positioned right-hand player serves first. A team's starting server belongs on the right at that team's even score and left at odd; partner is opposite. The server changes serving areas after each point won. Losing on first server passes to partner; losing on second server causes side-out. Store both starting-player identity and current server number. Court stacking tactics do not remove the correct-server/receiver requirements. [P1: 5.B]
- 2026 provisional rally scoring awards a point on every rally. Score call has two numbers. Doubles has ONE server per service rotation; a losing serving side gives up service immediately, and service after side-out begins from the correctly positioned player on the right according to the team's score. The server alternates areas after scoring while serving. Do not implement traditional first/second-server lives in this mode. [P1: 14.A]
- USA Pickleball's 2026 tournament rally option excludes doubles double-elimination, all 2026 Golden Ticket events and 2026 Nationals. The rulebook lists one-game and best-of-three/five rally formats to 11/15/21 and specific weather adjustments. These restrictions belong to the USA Pickleball-sanctioned profile, not an invented worldwide ban on other organisers' formats. [P1: 15.C.2]
- Important source conflict: an October 2025 USA Pickleball skills article contains a game-point service restriction/freeze description. The 2026 rulebook's 14.A.2 instead awards every rally and states no such exception. Use the adopted 2026 rulebook, not that older teaching article, for the launch implementation; keep any other league's freeze rule as an independently verified profile. [P1, P6]
## 2. Play rules and officiating inputs
- Serve diagonally into the correct service court, clear of the non-volley-zone line. Lines normally count in, but the non-volley-zone line belongs to that zone and a serve landing on it is a fault. A serve can touch the net and remain legal if it lands correctly; there is no automatic net-serve replay. [P1: 3.A, 7.E, 8]
- Volley serve: clear upward paddle arc, paddle head not above the highest wrist-joint part and ball no higher than waist at contact. Drop serve is released from natural unaided height without propulsion and hit after bouncing; volley-serve motion constraints are not copied onto it. Release visibility and no added release spin have separate provisions. Record the official's service-fault/replay reason rather than a generic second serve. [P1: 7]
- Two-bounce rule: serve must bounce before return, and the serve return must bounce on the serving side before being hit. Thereafter the normal volley/bounce choices apply, with return before a second bounce in standard play. Adapted wheelchair rules are separate. [P1: 10.A-10.B, 25]
- Non-volley zone: initiate volleys outside it. Contact by the volleying player or relevant equipment/clothing with the zone, or momentum causing prohibited contact, is a fault even after the ball becomes dead. A player who was in the zone must re-establish outside as specified before volleying. It is not a 'never enter the kitchen' rule: legal play after a bounce is different. [P1: 11]
- Faults include illegal service, two-bounce failures, out balls, prohibited contacts and non-volley-zone violations. Replays and referee corrections are distinct from faults. Players and officials have different call responsibilities in self-officiated/refereed/line-judge matches. Store who called, who confirmed, whether appealed/overruled, and the resulting event. [P1: 7-13, 17, 19-20]
- Calls relevant to the scorer include score, 'second server', 'side out', fault, replay, out, timeout and game/match. Do not assign an automatic score effect to a hand signal without the referee's confirmed call. The app should capture decisions, not infer a winner from a gesture. Questions/corrections about server, receiver or positions follow the adopted 2026 procedures, not a blanket assumption that every position error is an immediate penalty. [P1: 5.C, 8, 17, 19]
- Standard tournament timeouts: two per side per game to 11/15; three to 21; up to one minute each. In a single-game or tie-breaker game change ends at six for 11, eight for 15, eleven for 21, with the stated exception when game one was forfeited for late arrival. End-change does not change the server. Keep medical/referee/end-change breaks separate. [P1: 21]
- Technical foul: deduct one point from offending side; if its score is zero, add one to opponent. Correct player positions to the adjusted score; no server change or side-out. This can create a winning score regardless of prior server. Technical warnings, fouls, game/match forfeits have different consequences. [P1: 22]
## 3. Coaching and real academy structure in India
Dinkers Pickleball Academy advertises U12 junior, intermediate, advanced and pro batches. Its documented session durations are one hour for junior/intermediate, 1.5 hours for advanced and two hours for pro; programme content includes coordination/stroke mechanics, positioning/weekly match play, power/spin/mental work and pro video analysis. It also advertises private, semi-private and four-player group coaching. These are a concrete operator example, not national duration/price standards. [P3]
Its attendance policy allows one academy session a day with separate extra-session charges and no missed-session carry-forward. Accadon should support such policies as configurable entitlement/attendance rules, not silently assume makeup sessions. Morning/evening batches and weekly match play are distinct from one-off court bookings. [P3]
### Proposed configurable progression
The following is an Accadon coaching template, not an official grading certificate.
- Foundation: safe movement, ready position, paddle control, legal serve/return, two-bounce rule, non-volley-zone understanding and traditional scoring. Use cooperative rallies, target serves, short-court play and scoring practice.
- Developing: consistent dinks, third-shot drops, volleys, resets, court positioning and partner communication. Progress from controlled feeds to variable placement and constrained doubles points.
- Competitive: attack/defence transitions, third/fifth-shot choices, speed-up/counter decisions, stacking/receiving responsibilities and pressure games. Keep singles, doubles and mixed tactical goals separate.
- Performance: individual plans, video review, opponent plans, tournament formats, conditioning and recovery. Entry into local/state/national events depends on actual registration/selection; a coaching-level upgrade is not an external rating or federation selection. [P3-P5]
Proposed session sequence: readiness/warm-up, technical task, cooperative/target drill, conditioned points, match application and review. Academy chooses player/coach ratio, duration, weekly frequency and promotion thresholds. Record attempts/target hits, rally consistency, decision rubric, starting court, feed conditions, partner/task, assessor/video and next goal. Re-test comparable tasks; do not label an internal coach score as verified DUPR.
## 4. Tournament conduct and India-first practice
The USA Pickleball 2026 rulebook supports double elimination, single elimination with consolation, specified single elimination without consolation, round robin, pool play into elimination and roster-based team play. In its double-elimination format, a consolation winner who beats the undefeated finalist triggers a deciding match to 15. A generic bracket engine that never supports a reset/extra decider is insufficient. [P1: 15.B]
Round-robin ranking is match wins, then mutual record among tied sides, overall point differential, head-to-head point differential, differential against the next-highest ranked opponent, then total points, subject to the rule's unequal-pool restrictions. A withdrawal/retirement/forfeit removes that entrant's results from event standings under 15.B.4.a, although results still count toward rating. Persist standings eligibility separately from rating-export eligibility. [P1: 15.B.4]
Seeding is the responsibility of a draw/seeding committee; do not assume a universal Indian seed count. Common supported bracket capacities such as 8/16/32, groups of organiser-selected size and balanced seed distribution are software options, not mandatory laws. Keep rating snapshot/provider, seed, bye, pool, advancement, consolation path and approved partner changes. USA Pickleball allows a tightly scoped partner change before first-round play with director approval, not silent pair replacement after play begins. [P1: 15.E, 18.G]
Categories can combine discipline, age, gender and skill. Under USA Pickleball's profile, higher-rated partner determines skill division, while the younger partner determines an adult age-qualified pair's age category. Those are not automatically India's rules. Junior, adult, masters, mixed and open categories need their actual event-specific cutoffs, pair eligibility and combined-division policy. [P1: 15.A]
Indian evidence: secondary reporting on IPA Junior Nationals 2026 lists U12/U14/U16/U18 boys/girls; Rajasthan's 2026 trials report U12/U14/U16/U18/Open/30+/40+/50+/60+ and singles/doubles, with residency and entry-minimum conditions. These illustrate actual category variety but are not primary prospectuses and do not establish current registration availability or final draw/scoring. Verify the organiser's prospectus before configuring an actual event. [P7, P8]
Authority and ratings caveat: AIPA publishes its own rulebook; IPA maintains tournament/ranking pages; DUPR announces IPA verified-event reporting from 2026. These are different records. Secondary P8 also discusses PWR national ranking. Do not collapse ranking points, skill ratings, provider subscriptions or sanctioning identity into one field, and do not resolve Indian recognition/affiliation disputes from organisations' self-descriptions. Confirm the event's adopted authority and export destination. [P2, P4, P5, P8]
Scheduling: courts, player overlap across events, partner availability, rest policy, stage dependencies and actual duration. The USA Pickleball profile prohibits overlapping same-day event entry as specified and requires schedule-change notices. For a local academy, approve event overlap/rest explicitly. Publish draw/pools, court/order of play, game scores, retirement/forfeit status, final placings and separately labelled rating/ranking submissions. An exported result is not proof that an external rating has updated. [P1: 15.D, 16.L, 18.E; P4]
## 5. Data model and configuration
Product recommendations:
- RuleProfile: authority/document/edition/effective date, singles/doubles/team, side-out/rally, best-of/target/win-by/cap, initial server exception, servers-per-rotation, score-call arity, end-change trigger, timeouts, sanction arithmetic, approved adaptations and event exclusions.
- Entrant: athlete or pair ID with both athlete IDs; category eligibility/reference dates, affiliation, age/skill aggregation policy, provider-specific external IDs and frozen rating snapshot. Keep sanctioning body independent of rating provider.
- Match state: points/games, current server, starting server per side, server turn, right/left assignment, receiver, ends, replay/correction state, timeouts, technical warnings/fouls, status and approved winner.
- Event stream: rally winner, fault/reason, point awarded or service transition only, second-server/side-out, replay, correction, end change, penalty subtraction/addition, retirement/forfeit and official sign-off. Preserve actor, timestamp, correction chain and idempotency key.
- Tournament: pools/bracket, seed basis, byes, consolation/reset dependency, team rosters/tie rubbers, advancement, tie-break explanation, standings-result inclusion and rating-result inclusion. Export attempt/acknowledgement/external status are separate fields.
- Academy: programme, batch/slot, courts, staff, membership entitlement, attendance/makeup policy, coaching level per discipline, assessments, video, individual plan and guardian access. Court hire is not automatically a coaching entitlement.
Optional stats: serve/return outcomes, third-shot drop success, dink consistency, forced/unforced errors and partner combinations. These need coding definitions and observation, not just final game scores.
## 6. Acceptance tests and remaining gates
- Traditional opening 0-0-2: serving-side loss gives side-out at 0-0. Later first-server loss changes to second server without awarding receivers a point; second-server loss gives side-out. [P1]
- Provisional 2026 rally: receiving-side win adds a point and gives service with only one server in doubles; a receiving-side game-winning rally is not frozen by the older 2025 article. [P1, P6]
- At 10-10 in an 11-point win-by-two profile, 11-10 is unfinished; 12-10 wins. No invented 15-point cap. [P1]
- Serve on non-volley-zone line is fault; legal net-contact serve plays on. A momentum fault can be recognised after the rally appears over. [P1]
- Technical foul at zero awards opponent a point without side-out; ordinary foul at positive score deducts one and fixes positions. [P1]
- Round-robin retirement recalculates standings but preserves appropriate rating export; double elimination creates the deciding match where required. [P1]
Before official scoring, have a referee validate the adopted edition, service replay, penalties and bracket outcomes. Remaining choices: Indian event authority/primary prospectus, age/skill divisions, rating/ranking export requirements and API permission, rest policy and academy rubrics. No direct DUPR/PWR integration or federation approval is established by this research.
