# Accadon: Indian tournament methods and structures

Checked 15 October 2026. Covers table tennis, badminton, football, cricket and pickleball. This is a sourced catalog, not a claim to list every local event or every Indian sport. Actual Indian use, historical examples, rulebook permission and missing details are labeled separately.

Tournament method, match-scoring profile, entrant category and rating system are separate concepts. QF = quarterfinal; SF = semifinal; S = singles; D = doubles. A1 is pool A winner, A2 its runner-up. One team tie may contain several individual matches.

## Table tennis

### 1. Qualifying pools into single knockout

Actual national-ranking use: Kanpur 2026.

#### Structure

1. Ranked direct entrants: 16 senior / 8 other eligible events
2. Other entrants → pools of 3 or 4
3. One winner per pool → main knockout draw
4. Main draw → R16 → QF → SF → final

#### Details

Top-eight direct entry excludes U11. Pool of 3 gives 3 matches; pool of 4 gives 6. The prospectus does not fix a universal main-draw size. Direct entrants do not need invented pool scores.

Seeds: men's/women's ITTF world top 50 first, then national ranking; other events use national ranking. Seeds 1/2 opposite halves, later seed bands in prescribed positions. Same-association separation applies only in first-stage groups. Record the ranking snapshot and approved draw.

Most matches best of five games; senior semifinals/finals and U19 finals best of seven. The 2026 prospectus retains a clause heading saying 2025: referee confirmation is needed before operational use.

#### Sources

- [Kanpur 2026 prospectus](https://www.uptta.org/pdf/Prospectus-1st-National-Ranking-2026-Kanpur.pdf)

### 2. Team pools into progressive placement knockout

Actual Senior Nationals 2024, held January 2025; a dated example, not a universal current rule.

#### Structure

1. Teams → 8 round-robin groups
2. Top 2 per group → 16 teams
3. Progressive knockout → positions 1 through 16

#### Details

Tie: best of five singles, A-X, B-Y, C-Z, A-Y, B-X. Individual matches best of five games. Team seeds use the sum of latest ranking points of the best three players.

Progressive knockout includes placement branches after losses, not just a champion's bracket. This prospectus states 1-16 classification but does not supply every placement edge. Use referee-approved draw sheets rather than inventing the lower-position schedule.

Inter-Institutional 2025 offers another real shape: four groups/top two into an eight-team draw; same-group runners-up placed in the opposite half from their winner. Pool count is event-specific.

### 3. Direct knockout and school progression

Actual CBSE 2026-27 structure.

#### Structure

1. Cluster team knockout → winner + runner-up to Nationals
2. National team event → league-cum-knockout
3. Individual knockout → cluster top 4 to Nationals

#### Details

U14/U17/U19 boys/girls are categories, not different tournament algorithms. Team roster 3-4; order A-X/B-Y/C-Z/A-Y/B-X. CBSE has its own stage-specific match lengths.

CBSE's TT clauses do not fix one national pool size or qualifier count. These remain approved event inputs. Senior National and Inter-Institutional doubles examples use direct knockout, with each pair as one entrant.

#### Sources

- [CBSE 2026-27 rules, Table Tennis section](https://academic.cbseit.in/sports/documents/Rules_of_Sports_and_Games_(2026-27)_v4.pdf)

### 4. Single round robin with a top-four playoff

Actual professional league: UTT season 7, July 2026.

#### Structure

1. 7 franchises → each meets the other 6 once
2. League standings → top 4
3. 2 semifinals → final

#### Details

Calculated: 7 × 6 ÷ 2 = 21 league ties, then 3 playoff ties. The official announcement establishes this outer structure, not every internal tie-scoring or standings rule. Do not import an earlier season's score format.

### 5. Accelerated Swiss, singles and doubles

Actual local club use: Chennai Premier Clubs League seasons 8 and 9.

#### Structure

1. Skill bands → accelerated first rounds
2. Round 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5
3. Pair by running score; avoid repeats
4. Final standings; nobody eliminated

#### Details

Season 8 singles uses five rounds, 1 point win/0 loss, temporary extra pairing point for Elite players removed in round 3. An entrant receives at most one bye; that bye gives one tournament point and two weighted game points.

Tie-breaks: weighted game points, then head-to-head if played, then a tie-break match. A 2-0 win gives 3 weighted points; 2-1 gives winner 2/loser 1. These are local club rules, not TTFI ranking rules.

The page estimates 46 entrants and says 116 matches; 23 × 5 actually gives 115 before tie-breaks. Season 9 separately documents accelerated Swiss doubles and a five-pair ambassador round robin.

#### Sources

- [CPCLTT season 8 rules](https://www.cpcltt.com/season8-swiss-system/)
- [CPCLTT season 9, August 2025](https://www.cpcltt.com/cpcltt-season-9/)

### 6. Standings, seeding and required structure fields

Use the adopted ITTF/TTFI profile, not generic game difference.

#### Structure

1. Eligible results → group match points
2. Tied members → mutual-results mini-table and ordered ratios
3. Approved positions → qualifiers → locked draw

#### Details

The Original sport research view retains the current ITTF source: match points 2 win/1 played loss/0 unplayed or unfinished loss. Tied members use mutual results and applicable win/loss ratios of matches, games and points, with recalculation as members separate. Preserve lot/referee decisions.

Store group capacities, qualifiers, direct entries, ranking snapshot, seed bands, affiliation separation, byes, tie order, match length per stage, placement branches and correction history. No full double-elimination or challenge-ladder national TT template was established by this research.

#### Sources

- [ITTF 2026 Statutes, 3.7.5](https://documents.ittf.sport/sites/default/files/public/2026-02/2026_Statutes_v1_consolidated_clean.pdf)

### Worked structures (illustrations, not official event draws)

#### Eight-entry single knockout

QF1: 1 vs 8; QF2: 4 vs 5; QF3: 2 vs 7; QF4: 3 vs 6. SF1: winner QF1 vs winner QF2. SF2: winner QF3 vs winner QF4. Final: winner SF1 vs winner SF2. This illustrative seeded layout is not a substitute for a federation's mandated random placement or affiliation separation.

#### Four pools, top two: one cross-pool layout

A1-D2; B1-C2; C1-B2; D1-A2. This illustrative eight-qualifier layout avoids an immediate same-pool rematch. Quarterfinal-to-semifinal links and later rematch avoidance must also be declared. Do not use it where the competition specifies a different draw.

#### Fixture counts and byes

For N entrants: single round robin has N(N-1)/2 fixtures; double round robin has N(N-1); full single knockout has N-1 played fixtures if all are resolved normally. For a padded knockout, capacity B is the next power of two at least N; byes = B-N. Six entrants fit eight positions with two byes, not two fabricated scores. A four-entry pool has six fixtures; a five-entry pool ten. Withdrawals, no-shows, bronze/placement matches and reset finals change the played total.

## Badminton

### 1. Qualifying knockout into main knockout

Actual BAI senior-ranking event: Chennai, July 2026.

#### Structure

1. Qualifying draw → 32 singles / 16 doubles qualifiers
2. 30 ranked + 2 host + 32 qualifiers → 64 singles
3. 15 ranked + 1 host + 16 qualifiers → 32 doubles
4. R64/R32 → R16 → QF → SF → final

#### Details

MS/WS use 64-entry main draws; MD/WD/XD use 32. Full draws require 63 matches per singles event or 31 per doubles event. Qualifying draw size is unrestricted by this circular; qualifying rounds depend on actual entries.

Seed from the applicable BAI/BWF snapshot. BAI general regulations place seed 1 top/2 bottom, 3-4 in remaining quarters and later bands in allowed sections. Preserve current overrides, affiliation separation, withdrawals and reserves.

The circular uses 3x21, with authorised 3x15 qualifying extension to 21 for huge entries. This is not the BWF 2027 transition profile. Tournament method and scoring edition stay separate.

#### Sources

- [BAI Chennai 2026 circular](https://www.badmintonindia.org/download/tournaments/BAI%20Circular%20at%20chennai%202026.pdf)
- [BAI older/undated general competition regulations](https://badmintonindia.org/download/regulations/FINAL%20REVISED%20COMPETITION%20REGULATION.pdf)

### 2. School team knockout and national pools

Actual CBSE 2026-27 structure.

#### Structure

1. Cluster: knockout, or approved small-entry league-cum-knockout
2. Nationals: pools generally at least 4 teams
3. Top 2 per pool → knockout

#### Details

Team order Singles-Doubles-Singles; roster 2-5, U14/U17/U19 boys/girls. National individual singles and doubles are separate knockout events; no national mixed doubles is listed in these clauses.

Pool count follows reporting entries. Four pools of four/top two is an illustrative 16-team implementation, not a CBSE-fixed national size. It yields eight qualifiers, then QF/SF/final.

CBSE's knockout clauses specify 3x15 without deuce; an approved small-entry league-cum-knockout exception mentions 3x21 semifinals/final. These school modifications are not the universal BAI/BWF law.

#### Sources

- [CBSE 2026-27 rules, Badminton section](https://academic.cbseit.in/sports/documents/Rules_of_Sports_and_Games_(2026-27)_v4.pdf)

### 3. Inter-state team tie structure

Actual BAI tie-composition regulations 2025.

#### Structure

1. Senior: S1 → S2 → D1 → S3 → D2
2. First 3 match wins decide senior tie
3. Junior U19: S1 → D1 → S2; first 2 wins

#### Details

Senior men/women roster 6-10; junior boys/girls 3-7. Submitted strength order remains across ties. A player may play at most one singles and one doubles in the same tie. Play stops when the tie is decided.

This source defines the inside of a tie, not a universal outer pool/bracket. Obtain the actual inter-state/zonal prospectus for stage sizes and progression. Doubles is an entrant discipline, not a tournament algorithm.

#### Sources

- [BAI Inter-State Team Championships regulations 2025](https://www.badmintonindia.org/download/news/Regulations%20for%20Inter%20State%20Team%20Championships%202025.pdf)

### 4. University houses: pools into semifinals

Actual organiser-published structure: IIT Madras Paradox 2026.

#### Structure

1. 12 houses → 4 pools of 3
2. Each house plays 2 group ties
3. Pool winners × 4 → semifinals
4. Final + third-place match

#### Details

Calculated: 12 group ties + 2 semifinals + final + bronze match = 16 team ties. Each tie lists MS, WS, MD and XD. The FAQ says majority wins but does not resolve a 2-2 result across these four submatches. This is a configuration blocker, not permission to invent a fifth match.

Pool seeding, exact group tie-breaks and the 2-2 decider require organiser confirmation. This college example is different from the BAI five/three-match inter-state tie.

#### Sources

- [IIT Madras Paradox badminton structure and FAQ](https://www.iitmparadox.org/events/sports/87)

### 5. Academy round-robin leagues

Actual operator-described example: AIM Badminton Academy.

#### Structure

1. Entries/categories → round-robin fixtures
2. Each entrant meets others in its league
3. Standings → champion, or a declared playoff

#### Details

AIM describes a previous edition with 200+ players across singles, doubles and mixed categories in a round-robin league. This does not prove one 200-player pool, exact qualifiers or tie-breaks. Those details need an event prospectus.

Reliable Indian evidence of a fully specified badminton Swiss, double-elimination or challenge-ladder event was not established here. A local listing named PT Ladder calls itself round robin and marks its organiser unverified. Its title alone cannot prove a challenge algorithm.

## Football

### 1. Single or double round-robin league

Actual IWL 2025-26: eight teams, double round robin.

#### Structure

1. 8 clubs → first pass: 7 matches each
2. Second pass → same 7 opponents again
3. Final league standings → title

#### Details

Calculated: 14 matches per club and 56 league matches. The official announcement uses two phases and centralised Kolkata/Kalyani venues. Double round robin does not necessarily mean home/away venues.

Standings points and tie-breaks must come from that league's rules. A league-only title and a league followed by playoffs are different outcomes.

### 2. Pools into championship knockout

Actual Santosh Trophy 2025-26 final round; U17 Women 2026-27 variant.

#### Structure

1. Santosh: 12 teams → 2 pools of 6
2. Top 4 each → 8 quarterfinalists
3. QF → SF → final

#### Details

Santosh admission distinguishes host and previous winner/runner-up direct entries from nine earlier group winners. Admission is not seeding. Calculated for single round robin: 30 group matches plus 7 knockout matches, 5 pool matches per team.

AIFF U17 Women uses 16 teams/four groups of four/top two to QFs. Calculated: 24 group matches + 7 knockout matches = 31. Same final bracket, different pool workload and advancement rate.

### 3. Top-six playoff with two-leg semifinals

Actual historical ISL 2024-25 structure, not asserted as the current season.

#### Structure

1. League ranks 1 and 2 → semifinal byes
2. Ranks 3-6 → 2 single-leg knockouts
3. Winners + top 2 → home/away semifinals
4. Two aggregate winners → single final

#### Details

Store legs linked to one tie, aggregate score and the adopted equal-aggregate decider. Do not import away goals or extra time from another competition.

This is not full double elimination. Top-two teams skip a round, but do not receive a second life after losing the semifinal tie.

Actual CBSE and AIFF examples, with different match-law profiles.

1. CBSE cluster knockout → national pools/top 2 → knockout
2. Futsal: pools 4+3+3+3 → top 2 each → QFs

### 6. Group rankings, seeding and cross-group qualifiers

AIFF youth operations manual 2026-27; competition-specific, not universal football.

#### Structure

1. Points → mutual points → mutual goal difference → mutual goals
2. Then overall goal difference → overall goals → lots
3. Best-placed across pools → separate comparison rules

#### Details

Youth manual points are 3 win/1 draw/0 loss. Cross-group qualifiers have a separate appendix, including unequal-group adjustment. Do not compare raw totals across unequal schedules without the adopted rule.

CBSE instead starts pool ties with goal difference and then specifies lots when unresolved. No universal Indian seed count or draw size was established. Store approved draw method, stage graph, groups, qualifiers, legs, ranking order, separate shoot-outs, admission/age category and protest status.

## Cricket

### 1. Elite groups into quarterfinals

Actual BCCI 2026-27: Ranji Elite and Vijay Hazare Elite.

#### Structure

1. 32 teams → 4 groups of 8
2. 7 league matches each → top 2 each
3. QF: A1-C2 / B1-D2 / C1-A2 / D1-B2
4. SF: QF1-QF4 and QF2-QF3 → final

#### Details

Calculated: 4 × (8×7÷2) = 112 league matches, then 7 knockouts = 119 fixtures. Ranji is multi-day; Vijay Hazare is one-day. Identical tournament graphs do not mean identical points, duration or drawn/tied-match rules.

Both have a separate Plate group of six: five league matches per team, top two to final. Calculated: 15 group matches + final = 16. Promotion/relegation requires the season's adopted conditions.

Actual BCCI 2026-27: Syed Mushtaq Ali Elite.

#### Structure

1. 32 teams → 4 groups of 8

### 4. IPL top-four second-chance playoff

Actual IPL 2026 official structure.

#### Structure

1. Ranks 1 vs 2 → Qualifier 1 → winner to final
2. Ranks 3 vs 4 → Eliminator → loser out
3. Q1 loser vs Eliminator winner → Qualifier 2
4. Q1 winner vs Q2 winner → final

#### Details

Four playoff matches after 70 league matches. Only the top two get another route after losing Q1. This is a modified playoff, not full double elimination.

Store loser-to-next-match edges as well as winner edges, league-rank slots, venue/date and reserve/weather policy. Qualifier 1 is a playoff match, not a qualifying-entry draw. Do not silently replace this with ordinary semifinals.

### 4. Double elimination with reset decider

Permitted in AIPA 2024; not claimed as a verified event result.

#### Structure

1. First loss → losers/consolation bracket
2. Second loss → eliminated
3. Losers winner vs undefeated winner
4. If undefeated side loses → extra match to 15

#### Details

AIPA 12.C.3 allows the losers-bracket winner to win gold. If it beats the undefeated finalist, an extra tie-breaker match to 15 is required. Do not omit this reset or confuse it with bronze consolation.

Calculated full two-loss structure for N entries: 2N-2 matches if the undefeated finalist wins immediately, or 2N-1 with reset, absent withdrawals affecting played matches. Pool play can feed single or double elimination under 12.C.5; 12.C.6 also describes medal-round seeding from a non-pool round robin. Confirm the actual event's adopted edition and reset policy.
